# RSS Lobster

**Unplatform yourself.**

---

## The Problem

You post on platforms you don't control. You don't need them anymore. An AI agent publishes from your phone and RSS handles subscriptions.

## The Reframe

Own your website. Publish from your phone. Your RSS Lobster agent does the rest.

## How It Works

Buy a domain. `yourname.com`. $12/year. Publish a feed. Subscribe to others. Done.

## RSS Lobster

Message your Lobster on iMessage, Telegram, Signal, or WhatsApp. It builds HTML + RSS and deploys to your site in four seconds.

**Five types:** Micro, Post, Image, Carousel, Link.

**Drafts.** Managed by your Lobster, not committed. Ask to list drafts and pick up where you left off anytime.

**Files, not databases.** Your site is a git repo. Everything version-controlled.

## Cost

$12/year for a domain. Hosting and reader: free.

## What It's Not

Not a social network. Not a CMS. One job: **publish**. MIT license. Runs on your hardware. Forever.
